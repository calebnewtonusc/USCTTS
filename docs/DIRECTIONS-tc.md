# Three directions for T Combinator

Written 2026-10-03, after CRAFT-tc.md and before any component. Each differs
in kind. The reader decides between them, not taste.

## A. The scoping sheet (chosen)

**Stance.** The page is a one-page brief we drafted for your company before
you got here. On `/tc/for/<slug>` it is filled from your YC listing; on `/tc`
it asks you to name your company so it can fill itself.

**Refuses.** Serif type of any kind. Monospace of any kind, including
uppercase tracked kickers. Cards and card grids. Logos of anyone but the
founder's own company. Any accent use that does not mean "this came from your
company's data."

**Type.** One family, Archivo, with width as the hierarchy: expanded (wdth 125) at weight 560 for display, normal width for reading. No second face.

**Colour.** Cool graphite ground, paper-white ink. One accent, a highlighter
yellow-green, and it has exactly one meaning: a value read from the founder's
listing. The CTA is paper-white, never the accent, so the accent can never be
confused with "click me."

**Signature.** The brief fills in. Each field from their listing gets a
highlighter stroke as it lands, and the work we would take, plus the work we
would refuse, is reasoned from their own tags with the reason shown.

**Motion.** Timed and deliberate, like a pen: a highlight sweeps left to right
under each filled value, staggered, total under 2.2s. Scroll moves one thin
progress rule in the margin. Nothing bounces, nothing floats.

## B. The letter

**Stance.** A personal letter from Caleb, one column, signed, with the company
name in the salutation. **Refuses** headings, sections, buttons other than a
reply link, imagery, data. **Type** a book serif for the letter, a grotesk
for the footer. **Colour** warm paper and ink, no accent. **Signature** the
salutation and one paragraph rewrite around the company. **Motion** none.

Lost because the TTS site is the editorial serif one, and because a letter
can only assert. It cannot show the reasoning that proves we read their
listing.

## C. The poster

**Stance.** One giant typographic poster per company: the company name at
200px, the offer in three lines, a phone-sized CTA. **Refuses** body copy
over 40 words, proof sections, scrolling past two screens. **Type** a
condensed grotesk at weight 800. **Colour** solid saturated field per
industry. **Signature** the name at poster scale. **Motion** a hard cut
entrance.

Lost because a skeptical founder reads a poster as marketing, and the honest
read in `00-THE-MESSAGE.md` says the first doubt is "student club wants
something." Volume does not answer that; specifics do.

## The choice

A founder at 60/40 trusts fastest the thing that shows we already did the
first ten minutes of work on their company, and the sheet makes that work
visible field by field, including the parts we would refuse. The letter and
the poster both ask to be believed; the sheet can be checked against their
own listing in the time it takes to read it.
