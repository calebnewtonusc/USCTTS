# Every creative direction for usctts.com, rated

Written 2026-10-05, after a day and a night of Caleb's reactions. Each rating
is out of 10 against four things together:

- his bar: a USC student thinks "HOLY SHIT I gotta join"
- the feel he named: Lemma's future, Gavin's young and alive, Clay's
  playground
- whether we can actually build it to that quality
- what he has already said about something like it

His own words are quoted where they decide a rating.

## 1. The one idea the whole site hangs on

| Direction                                                                                  | Rating | Why                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The machine: a lead travels through a contraption students built, and comes out a customer | 9      | It's literally "something getting built/transforming", his ask. It's Clay's proven language, it shows AI implementation as cause and effect, and one object can carry the whole page |
| LA as the internet: the city grid of light, with work streaming out from USC (current v4)  | 7      | It's "lives on the internet, centered on LA", his answer, and the opening looks like the future. But the deep dive drifted into panels he could call a slideshow                     |
| The week getting lighter: one business's calendar emptying                                 | 6      | Business owners love it. The freshman review says it makes a dentist the hero for six screens, and students lose the thread                                                          |
| The comeback: a dead club rebuilt in three months                                          | 6      | It's true and human, and it works on /members. It's too small to carry home                                                                                                          |
| The lab: a futuristic workspace where you watch AI get built                               | 5      | It looks like every AI startup and doesn't say what's different about TTS                                                                                                            |
| A journey across LA neighbourhoods, Gavin-style flight between places                      | 5      | Strong for Gavin because it's his life. For a club the places are invented, which makes it decoration                                                                                |

**Pick:** the machine as the heart, set inside the LA grid world we already have.
The grid is the opening and the stage. The machine is what you watch get built.

## 2. What the visuals are made of

| Direction                                                                                      | Rating                             | Why                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-rendered Blender/Nalana film with real light, clay materials and grain, scrubbed by scroll | 9                                  | It's the only way to reach the quality in the Clay loops he downloaded. Nalana is ours, and the M4 Pro renders it. It's heavier to ship, but under 8MB is fine |
| Real-time WebGL points, lines and shader fields (the v4 grid)                                  | 7                                  | It reads as the future, is light to ship, and works well for maps and data. It can't do objects without looking 1995                                           |
| Real-time three.js modelled objects                                                            | 2                                  | He called it "1995", "1980 ah" and "Clay would never make smth this ugly with no point". Three attempts proved it                                              |
| Manim diagrams                                                                                 | 7 as support, 3 as the main visual | They're great for teaching how the AI works inside the x-ray, but too flat to be the spectacle                                                                 |
| Real photos and video of members                                                               | 8 later                            | Nothing beats real people for "I want to join". We have none yet                                                                                               |
| AI-generated video (Higgsfield)                                                                | 4                                  | He said no to spending credits, and it's hard to keep on-brand                                                                                                 |
| Flat 2D illustration or SVG motion                                                             | 5                                  | Clean, but reads as a template next to Clay's 3D                                                                                                               |

**Pick:** the Blender/Nalana film for the machine, the WebGL grid for the world,
and manim inside the x-ray. Swap in real footage the moment it exists.

## The combination these ratings point to

1. The opening: the LA grid assembles, and the camera dives into the machine in
   the middle of the city.
2. Clay and Perplexity are parts of the machine, with the valuations beside
   them.
3. The deep dive: one continuous scrubbed film of a lead travelling through
   the machine students built. You're the builder and the business is the
   client. Manim lives in the x-ray.
4. The ending: two doors on cardinal, plus a real next step once there's a
   date.
