# TTS copy, TroyLabs' clarity in our voice

Caleb, 2026-10-04: "troylabs has solid copy but their site is shit", and he
pointed at https://usctroylabs.com/build. The text is in
`docs/ref/troylabs-home.txt` and `docs/ref/troylabs-build.txt`. What their
copy does right:

- it says what they are in one sentence
- it lists initiatives with one plain line each
- it shows real numbers
- it gives each division a one-line job
- it shows a numbered timeline for the program
- it names the people

Take that structure. Keep our own voice and our own facts. Never name
TroyLabs or any other club.

**Facts we can use** (all in `docs/POSITIONING.md` or `data/people.ts`):

- Caleb Newton and Tyler Larsen are co-presidents, and Emily Zhao leads
  design.
- 15 alumni, now at Apple, Bloomberg, Reddit, Capital One and more.
- 6 advisors, including McKinsey and Google.
- The club was dormant with zero members inherited, and was rebuilt in
  three months.
- The apply form's three tracks: Building, Consulting, Growing.
- T Combinator works with three YC companies each spring.
- The run on the home page used real data, on 2026-09-15.

**Facts we can't use:** member counts, majors counts, clients, dollar
figures, or "weekly" unless the meetings data shows it.

Voice: plain, contracted and short, like Caleb talking. No slogans, no
"X. Then Y." couplets.

## Home

- **Hero H1:** `USC's GTM and AI club.`
- **Hero body:** `We learn to build the systems companies run on: data sets, GTM engines and AI agents. Then we build them for real companies.`
- **Buttons:** `Apply to join` and `See how it works`.

**What we do**, three initiatives, one line each, with a small figure each
in the shared hairline family:

- `Build sessions`: `Hands-on nights where every member ships a working piece of the machine.`
- `Practice builds`: `Real pipelines on public data, like the one running on this page.`
- `T Combinator`: `Our team that works with YC companies. Three a semester.` (link: the T Combinator site)

**Numbers band** (TroyLabs' stat row, our sourced numbers):

- `15` / `alumni at Apple, Bloomberg, Reddit, Capital One and more`
- `6` / `advisors, including McKinsey and Google`
- `0 to a full roster` / `in three months, after we took the club over dormant`

**Tracks** (the apply form's three, each with its job):

- `Building`: `Engineering and AI agents. You ship the tools.`
- `Consulting`: `GTM engineering for real companies: lists, enrichment, outbound, the CRM.`
- `Growing`: `Marketing, content and design that gets the work seen.`

**Who runs it:** `Caleb Newton and Tyler Larsen, co-presidents. Emily Zhao, design.` Then the roster as now, McKinsey first.

**Join band:** `Join TTS, learn to build all of this, and build it for a real company.` / button `Apply to join`.

## `/build`, the program page, in TroyLabs' timeline shape

- **H1:** `How a semester at TTS works`
- **Timeline**, numbered and one line each:
  1. `Apply`: `Tell us your track and why. We read every one.`
  2. `Learn the machine`: `Build sessions take you through data, enrichment, qualification, prompting and agents.`
  3. `Practice build`: `Your team builds a real pipeline on public data, start to finish.`
  4. `Real work`: `The strongest teams take on real company work, including T Combinator's three YC companies each spring.`
  5. `Show it`: `End the semester with something running that you built.`
- Then the session list and the slides, as now.
- No numbers on this page except sourced ones.

## Everywhere

Every line should answer a student's question:

- what is this?
- what would I do?
- who's in it?
- how do I join?

If a line sounds like a tagline, rewrite it as a plain sentence you'd say to
someone's face.
