TroyLabs

Where USC student builders launch, scale, and raise capital.

TroyLabs is a student-run organization at the University of Southern California, proudly sponsored by the Lloyd Greif Center for Entrepreneurship and Marshall School of Business. We're dedicated to fostering entrepreneurship and supporting early-stage startups at USC. Our mission is to encourage innovation, empower student founders, and promote entrepreneurial curiosity across campus, creating a vibrant ecosystem where USC startups can flourish and make a lasting impact.

OUR THREE INITIATIVES
BUILD

Our premier 8-week accelerator for startups. Mentorship, resources, and consulting across product, tech, design, marketing, and finance.

IGNITE

Our general membership program helps you grow entrepreneurial skills through fireside chats, workshops, and networking events with founders and investors.

DEMO

SoCal’s largest student-run entrepreneurship conference. 50+ startups, 1000+ attendees, pitch competitions, and workshops.
